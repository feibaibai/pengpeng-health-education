About Us

平台介绍

Platform Introduction

蓬蓬科普是由重庆大学附属肿瘤医院药学部牵头创建的创新性医药科普自媒体平台。 我们致力于为老年肿瘤患者及其家属提供专业、易懂、个性化的用药科普信息。

Pengpeng Health is an innovative medication education platform initiated by the Department of Pharmacy at Chongqing University Cancer Hospital. We are dedicated to providing professional, easy-to-understand, and personalized medication education for elderly cancer patients and their families.

平台采用"医学+人工智能"跨学科融合模式，依托三大AI模型——DeepSeek、ChatGPT、Grok构建知识矩阵， 通过智能科普引擎、场景化内容矩阵和精准传播网络，为用户提供高质量的医药科普服务。

Our platform features an innovative "Medicine + AI" interdisciplinary model, leveraging three major AI models—DeepSeek, ChatGPT, and Grok—to build a comprehensive knowledge matrix. Through intelligent education engines, scenario-based content, and precision delivery networks, we provide high-quality medication education services.

项目背景

Project Background

随着人口老龄化加剧，老年肿瘤患者数量逐年增加。这一群体面临着多病共存、多重用药、生理功能衰退等复杂情况， 对用药安全性和有效性的需求尤为迫切。

With the aging of the population, the number of elderly cancer patients is increasing annually. This group faces complex challenges including multiple comorbidities, polypharmacy, and declining physiological function, creating urgent needs for medication safety and efficacy.

然而，传统的用药教育方式往往存在信息碎片化、专业性过强、缺乏针对性等问题。 蓬蓬科普平台应运而生，旨在通过科技手段，将专业的医药知识转化为老年人易于理解和接受的形式， 并根据个人情况提供个性化推荐，真正实现"精准科普"。

However, traditional medication education methods often suffer from fragmented information, excessive technical jargon, and lack of personalization. Pengpeng Health was created to address these gaps by using technology to transform professional pharmaceutical knowledge into formats that are accessible to elderly patients, and providing personalized recommendations based on individual circumstances to achieve true "precision education."

核心技术

Core Technologies

1. 知识图谱构建

1. Knowledge Graph Construction

系统化梳理药物信息、疾病信息、症状、不良反应、相互作用等实体及其关系，形成结构化知识网络， 为精准检索和智能推荐奠定基础。

Systematically organizing entities such as drug information, diseases, symptoms, adverse reactions, and interactions, forming a structured knowledge network that enables precise retrieval and intelligent recommendations.

2. 分面检索技术

2. Faceted Search

基于知识图谱提供多维度、层级化信息筛选，用户可同时按肿瘤类型、药物类别、症状等多个维度缩小搜索范围， 帮助用户快速定位所需科普内容。

Multi-dimensional, hierarchical filtering based on the knowledge graph. Users can narrow searches by tumor type, drug category, symptoms, and more simultaneously, helping them quickly locate relevant content.

3. 机器学习推荐

3. ML-Based Recommendations

分析用户个人情况和浏览行为，构建用户画像，利用协同过滤、基于内容的推荐等算法实现个性化内容推送。

Analyzing user profiles and browsing behavior to construct user profiles, and using collaborative filtering and content-based algorithms for personalized content delivery.

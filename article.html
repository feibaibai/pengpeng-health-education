老年肿瘤患者用药注意事项

发布时间：2024年1月15日 Published: January 15, 2024 | 阅读时间：约5分钟 Reading time: ~5 minutes

老年肿瘤患者由于生理功能下降、合并疾病多、多重用药等情况，在用药过程中需要特别注意以下事项：

Elderly cancer patients need to pay special attention to the following medication considerations due to declined physiological function, multiple comorbidities, and polypharmacy:

一、药物剂量调整

1. Dosage Adjustment

老年人肝肾功能减退，药物代谢和排泄能力下降，容易发生药物蓄积。因此，应根据肝肾功能情况调整药物剂量。 通常建议从较低剂量开始，逐渐滴定至有效剂量。

Elderly patients often have declined liver and kidney function, leading to reduced drug metabolism and excretion, which can cause drug accumulation. Therefore, drug dosages should be adjusted based on liver and kidney function. It is generally recommended to start with a lower dose and titrate gradually to an effective dose.

二、药物相互作用

2. Drug Interactions

老年肿瘤患者常同时使用多种药物，包括抗肿瘤药物、支持治疗药物、合并疾病治疗药物等，容易发生药物相互作用。 用药前应告知医生所有正在使用的药物，包括处方药、非处方药和保健品。

Elderly cancer patients often use multiple medications simultaneously, including anti-cancer drugs, supportive care medications, and medications for comorbidities, increasing the risk of drug interactions. Before starting any new medication, inform your doctor about all medications you are taking, including prescription drugs, over-the-counter medications, and supplements.

三、不良反应监测

3. Adverse Reaction Monitoring

老年患者对药物不良反应的耐受性较差，容易出现严重不良反应。 应密切监测血常规、肝肾功能等指标，及时发现和处理不良反应。 常见不良反应如骨髓抑制、恶心呕吐、肝肾损伤等在老年患者中可能更为严重。

Elderly patients have poorer tolerance to adverse drug reactions and are more prone to severe adverse reactions. Close monitoring of blood counts, liver and kidney function is essential to detect and manage adverse reactions promptly. Common adverse reactions such as bone marrow suppression, nausea and vomiting, and liver and kidney damage may be more severe in elderly patients.

四、用药依从性

4. Medication Adherence

老年患者可能因认知功能下降、记忆力减退等原因导致用药依从性不佳。 建议使用分药盒、设置用药提醒，或请家属协助管理药物。

Elderly patients may have poor medication adherence due to cognitive decline or memory impairment. It is recommended to use pill organizers, set medication reminders, or seek assistance from family members in managing medications.

五、生活方式配合

5. Lifestyle Support

合理的饮食、适当的运动、充足的休息对老年肿瘤患者尤为重要。 这些生活方式的配合可以提高治疗效果，减轻药物不良反应，改善生活质量。

Proper diet, appropriate exercise, and adequate rest are particularly important for elderly cancer patients. These lifestyle modifications can enhance treatment efficacy, reduce adverse drug reactions, and improve quality of life.
